Before You Buy

Common Questions

The three we get asked most. Ten more, with their sourcing, on the full FAQ.

What size BMX do I need for my height?

On freestyle the wheel is 20 inch whatever your height, and the size you pick is the top tube. Race sizes do map to height, but the published charts disagree by up to six inches on the same size name.

Chromoly or hi-ten, is it worth it?

Yes for anything you land on. Chromoly is about twice as strong for the same tube weight. It is rarely all or nothing though, and most bikes step up a piece at a time.

How much should a first bike cost?

Check the spec sheet rather than the price. At the same money one brand sells you an all hi-ten bike and another sells you a chromoly one.
