Your frame's shell diameter sets the bearing outer diameter. Your cranks set the bore. You need both to match, and the guide walks through measuring each one.

14mm, 3/8 inch, or 10mm axle?
These are three different sizes, not three names for the same one. 3/8 inch is 9.525mm, which is not a 10mm axle. Measure before ordering pegs or a replacement.

Will this sprocket fit your cranks?
Sprocket bore and spindle diameter have to match, and plenty of sprockets are bored for the largest size with an adapter dropping them down. Check the bore before you buy.

Threadless or threaded front end?
A quill stem goes inside the steerer, a threadless stem clamps around the outside. They do not swap without changing the fork. The guide covers telling them apart.

Pivotal, railed, or tripod?
The seat and the post have to use the same mounting system, and post diameter has to match your frame's seat tube. Both are quick to check with the bike in front of you.
